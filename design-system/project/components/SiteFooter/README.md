# SiteFooter

The bottom bar: the year and the author on the left, then muted ContactLinks and an RSS link, under a `border` rule.

**Consumer provides:** nothing.

- Text is `body-sm` in `muted-foreground`. The links use the `meta` mono style and turn `foreground` on hover.
- `space-6` padding inside the `content-width` column. Items wrap with a 16px column gap.
- No copyright symbol: just the year and the name, joined by a non-breaking space.
